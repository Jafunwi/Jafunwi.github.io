About the Jovial Albatross

The Jovial Albatross Wind Tea Cart is run by John Afunwi, a computer science student who likes building things and sharing them. The cart is named after a mascot: a cheerful albatross that rides the wind instead of fighting it.

That idea shapes everything we serve. Our four iced teas are named after ocean winds, and each one is brewed small-batch with simple ingredients. We keep the menu short on purpose, so every cup gets proper attention.

Whether you are heading to the beach or just passing by, we hope the cart gives you a good reason to slow down for a few minutes.
