Where to Find the Cart

The Jovial Albatross Wind Tea Cart sets up on the boardwalk, just steps from the sand. Look for the teal cart with the coral stripe and a little albatross painted on the side. If you can hear the waves, you are close.

The cart stays in one spot at the north end of the boardwalk, near the public beach entrance. There is a bike rack beside it, and parking is a short walk away in the public lot. The area is flat and easy to reach on foot, by bike, or with a stroller.

On busy weekends we may move a few steps to make room for the line, but you will always be within sight of the boardwalk entrance.
