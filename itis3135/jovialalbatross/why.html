Why

Tea is one of the simplest ways to slow down. It takes a few minutes to drink, it tastes good cold on a hot day, and it pairs well with a view. A seaside cart gives people a reason to stop, look at the water, and relax.

We chose four winds instead of a long menu because a short menu is easier to do well. Each tea has its own mood, from the calm of Doldrums to the kick of Gale, so there is something for most people without any clutter.

The albatross is the reason for the whole idea. It travels the world on air currents with very little effort, and we like to think a good cup of iced tea can give you that same feeling for a moment.
