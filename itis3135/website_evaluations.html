Website Evaluations

Apple

  • Contrast: Strong. Large, bold headlines stand out against plain backgrounds, and the main call-to-action buttons are easy to spot.
  • Repetition: Strong. The same fonts, button style, spacing and navigation bar repeat on every page, so the site feels like one product.
  • Alignment: Strong. Text and images sit on a clean grid, mostly centered, with nothing floating at random.
  • Proximity: Strong. Each product's name, tagline and links are grouped together, with generous white space separating one product from the next.

Ling's Cars

  • Contrast: Poor. Bright clashing colors and many competing text sizes make everything shout, so nothing stands out as most important.
  • Repetition: Poor. Fonts, colors and button styles change constantly, so there is no consistent visual identity.
  • Alignment: Poor. Text, images and banners are scattered at different positions and angles with no clear grid.
  • Proximity: Poor. Unrelated items are packed together, and related information is spread out, so it is hard to tell what belongs with what.
